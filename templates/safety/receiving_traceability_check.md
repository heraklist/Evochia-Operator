# Receiving & Traceability Check — OPERATIONS

- Date/time and responsible person
- Supplier and item
- Lot/batch / date coding where available
- Packaging integrity and condition
- Temperature/transport condition where relevant
- Allergen/specification match and substitution check
- Accept / reject / quarantine
- Corrective action
- Traceability record location
- Source/requirement used if a legal criterion is applied
- Human review for exceptions or incident escalation
