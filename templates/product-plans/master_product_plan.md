> This is the single development plan. It defines what the product is, who it serves, how it operates, what is known, what must be proven and the next practical action. **Do not lock pricing** or approve launch before the relevant evidence gates are satisfied.

## Decision States

- `LOCKED` — approved decision; change only with documented revision.
- `UNDER_VALIDATION` — hypothesis requiring real execution/customer/economic evidence.
- `OPEN` — deliberately unresolved.
- `PENDING` — dependent on an earlier gate.
