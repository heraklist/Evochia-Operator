# Product Pilot Evidence Pack

## 1. Pilot identity
Product/version, date/location, audience, paid/unpaid, operating team, scope and hypotheses.

## 2. Evidence hierarchy
Interpret evidence in this order unless the product context gives a stronger reason:

1. **Observed behavior** — what guests/users actually did, accepted, completed, repeated or avoided.
2. **Specific intent** — concrete request, booking intent, willingness to pay, referral or next-step commitment.
3. **Comparative preference** — chosen over a credible alternative or clearly preferred version.
4. **General feedback** — descriptive comments without committed behavior.
5. **Polite praise** — useful for tone, weak as product-market evidence.

Do not promote polite praise into proof of demand.

## 3. Hypothesis results
For each hypothesis: expected signal, observed signal, evidence, confidence, pass/fail/inconclusive, required iteration.

## 4. Operational evidence
Prep hours, service time, staffing, equipment, venue friction, failure/recovery, safety issues, repeatability, cleanup and transport.

## 5. Economic evidence
Record **actual cost**, founder labour, third-party cost, capacity used, achievable throughput, minimum viable price inputs and **opportunity cost**. Keep cash cost distinct from economic cost.

## 6. Customer evidence
Observed behavior, specific intent, comparative preference, general feedback and polite praise kept in separate fields.

## 7. Quality / culinary evidence
Guest response, technical execution, dish/experience bottlenecks, consistency and non-negotiable quality standards.

## 8. Decision recommendation
Recommend only one of: iterate, repeat pilot, proceed to paid validation, prepare commercial packaging, hold, or stop. State which decision register entries can move state and why.
