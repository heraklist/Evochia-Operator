# Evochia Commercial Policy

**Policy status:** `OWNER_REVIEW_DRAFT`
**Canonical effect:** none until owner approval.
**Unresolved rules:** `NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL` unless explicitly marked otherwise.

## 1. Core commercial model

**Working rule — `CANDIDATE_FROM_OWNER_WORKING_DECISION`:** Evochia sells a professional service. Food/ingredient and third-party supplier costs are normally transparent pass-through at actual cost/receipt rather than a hidden food markup. Historical proposals support this operating pattern, but each service may need an approved exception policy.

Commercial evaluation must still calculate the full economic event result before a client fee is proposed:

`food + founder/chef labour + assistants/service + prep/shopping + travel + accommodation + equipment + consumables + overhead + opportunity cost`

The client-facing price is then governed by approved Evochia policy, not by a generic target-food-cost shortcut.

## 2. Cost and fee boundaries

- Food / groceries / third-party supplier purchases: separate actual-cost evidence where the service model calls for pass-through.
- Founder labour: never zero in INTERNAL economic analysis.
- Additional staff, equipment/rentals, travel, accommodation and unusual logistics: model explicitly; inclusion/exclusion must be stated in the quote.
- Promotions or agency arrangements must not erase the INTERNAL economic result. Any deliberate promo subsidy is recorded as a business decision.
- Discounting below the calculated minimum viable price requires explicit owner decision and a stated strategic reason. `NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL`.

## 3. VAT / tax presentation

Do **not** hardcode a universal VAT rate from a historical proposal. Apply the **current applicable** tax profile for the actual service, date, entity and jurisdiction, and show whether quoted figures are net or gross. Tax-sensitive claims require current verification when material.

## 4. Service-specific pricing

Current candidate rates live in `current_rates.md`. That file distinguishes working owner decisions from `PROPOSAL_SPECIFIC_EVIDENCE`, historical examples and unresolved conflicts.

## 5. Quote governance

A quote should state:
- service scope and guest/service assumptions;
- what is included/excluded;
- food/supplier-cost treatment;
- staffing and logistics assumptions;
- VAT/tax presentation;
- deposit/payment terms from the approved terms policy;
- change triggers that can cause repricing.

Historical proposals and bookings may teach structure, tone and risk cases. They **must not** silently become current policy.
