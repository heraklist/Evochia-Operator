# Evochia Terms Policy

**Policy status:** `OWNER_REVIEW_DRAFT`
Historical booking terms are evidence, not automatically universal terms.

## Booking and deposit

**Candidate default:** 30% deposit to secure date/team/planning. `NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL` for company-wide use.

A villa booking used a 30% deposit, non-refundable treatment once paid, and linked it to securing dates, team, planning and logistics. That is `PROPOSAL_SPECIFIC_EVIDENCE` until the owner confirms a general rule.

## Balance payment

`NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL`. Existing evidence varies by service model: household collaboration used monthly advance; an event booking used remaining balance before service unless otherwise agreed. The canonical policy must define defaults by service family rather than force one rule onto all work.

## Cancellation and changes

`NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL`.

Candidate principles from booking evidence:
- deposit may become non-refundable after confirmation;
- late cancellation may expose the client to committed staffing, accommodation, supplier or preparation costs;
- material changes to date, guest count, venue, staffing, menu or service scope may require a revised quote/written adjustment.

Do not automatically generalize the historical “less than 14 days” rule until approved.

## Quote validity

`NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL`. Define a standard quote validity period and a shorter validity/repricing rule for volatile supplier/travel costs. Until approved, every quote must state its own validity explicitly.

## Ingredients and supplier costs

Candidate working rule: actual ingredient/grocery/third-party costs remain separate where stated and are evidenced by receipts/expense summary. If Evochia advances purchases, reimbursement timing must be stated in the quote/service agreement.

## VAT / taxes

Use the **current applicable** VAT/tax treatment for the specific service/date and state clearly whether figures are net or gross. A historical 24% calculation is not a universal tax rule.

## Travel, accommodation, staff and equipment

Inclusions/exclusions must be explicit. Extra staffing, special equipment/rentals, travel/ferry/tolls/parking, accommodation and third-party styling or services are quoted separately unless specifically included.

## Force majeure / external disruption

Candidate principle: good-faith practical solution for circumstances outside reasonable control, while preserving safety and already-committed third-party costs where applicable. `NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL` for final legal wording.

## Acceptance

Final booking method, written acceptance threshold, deposit condition and signature/email confirmation model are `NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL` and may vary by service family.
