# Evochia Terms Policy

**Policy status:** `OWNER_REVIEW_DRAFT`
Historical booking terms remain evidence, not automatically universal terms. This file is prepared for owner review and does not gain canonical effect until explicit approval.

## 1. Booking and deposit

**Prepared rule — `CANDIDATE_FROM_OWNER_WORKING_DECISION`:** booking confirmation requires **written acceptance + deposit**.

Prepared deposit amount: **30% of the fixed confirmed booking value**.

The fixed confirmed booking value may include agreed fixed service, staffing, transport or equipment lines that are already final at the time of booking. It excludes variable/pass-through groceries, supplier purchases and other actual costs that are not yet fixed.

Third-party deposits or prepayments that Evochia must commit in advance may be requested separately from the 30% booking deposit where stated in the quote/agreement.

**Refundability remains OPEN.** Approval of the 30% booking requirement does not imply a universal non-refundable rule or cancellation window.

## 2. Balance payment

`NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL` / `OPEN`.

Existing evidence varies by service model. Until a company-wide default is approved, every quote/service agreement that requires balance payment must state its own balance timing explicitly.

Do not infer a universal “before service”, “on service day”, “monthly advance” or other rule from historical proposals.

## 3. Cancellation, refundability and changes

`NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL` / `OPEN` for company-wide cancellation windows and deposit refundability.

Until approved, each quote must define its own material cancellation/refundability terms where relevant.

Prepared general principles that may be expressed only when the specific quote supports them:

- committed third-party staffing, accommodation, supplier or preparation costs may become non-recoverable;
- material changes to date, guest count, venue, staffing, menu, transport or service scope may trigger written repricing or revised scope;
- historical “less than 14 days” wording is not canonical.

## 4. Quote validity

`NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL` / `OPEN` for a company-wide standard validity period.

Until approved, each CLIENT-SAFE quote must state its own validity explicitly. A quote with no canonical default cannot silently imply indefinite validity.

## 5. Ingredients and supplier costs

Prepared working rule: when groceries/ingredients/third-party purchases are pass-through, they remain separate actual-cost items supported by receipts/expense evidence. If Evochia advances purchases, reimbursement/prepayment treatment must be explicit in the quote/service agreement.

An all-in/per-person/package quotation may present these costs differently to the client, but INTERNAL costing remains granular under `commercial_policy.md`.

## 6. VAT / taxes

Prepared rule: use the **current applicable** VAT/tax treatment for the specific service/date/entity/jurisdiction and state whether relevant quoted figures are net/gross and VAT included/excluded.

A historical 24% calculation is not a universal rule.

## 7. Travel, accommodation, staff and equipment

Inclusions/exclusions must be explicit. Extra staffing, special equipment/rentals, travel/ferry/tolls/parking, accommodation and third-party styling/services are separate unless specifically included in the chosen quotation format.

Service uplifts for yacht/island/remote disruption do not silently replace actual transport/ticket/accommodation lines.

## 8. Provisional transport and repricing gate

A transport line marked **`TRANSPORT_UNVERIFIED`** is provisional and cannot be treated as a final verified transport commitment.

The proposal must disclose that the line is subject to transport verification and possible repricing. Verification must occur **before final acceptance / booking confirmation**.

After verification:

- the final client transport line must respect the verified-cost floor defined in `staffing_policy.md`;
- a charge below the verified INTERNAL transport cost requires explicit `OWNER_APPROVED_PROMO_SUBSIDY`;
- an unverified low estimate does not become a retroactive subsidy automatically.

A client may receive or review a provisional proposal, but final booking confirmation requires a final quote whose material transport terms are no longer unresolved.

## 9. Multi-day travel model

Where material, the quote must identify whether the assignment uses the **Stay model** or **Commute model** from `staffing_policy.md`. These are mutually exclusive for the same service period unless an exceptional mixed arrangement is explicitly described and economically justified.

## 10. Force majeure / external disruption

Final legal wording remains `NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL`.

Prepared principle: seek a good-faith practical solution for circumstances outside reasonable control while preserving food safety, legal obligations and already-committed third-party costs where applicable.

## 11. Per-quote material-terms rule

While company-wide defaults remain unresolved, a CLIENT-SAFE quote may proceed only when it explicitly states the material terms relevant to that booking and does not present an unresolved company default as canonical.

Depending on the quote, material terms may include:

- quote validity;
- deposit requirement/status;
- balance timing;
- cancellation/refundability wording;
- transport verification state;
- material repricing triggers;
- VAT/net-gross presentation.

The absence of a company-wide default is not itself a blocker when the quote supplies its own explicit term. A missing or ambiguous material quote term remains a blocker.

## 12. Items intentionally still OPEN

- balance-payment timing;
- cancellation windows;
- deposit refundability;
- standard quote-validity period;
- peak-date-specific terms/surcharges where applicable;
- final force-majeure/legal wording beyond the prepared operating principle.
