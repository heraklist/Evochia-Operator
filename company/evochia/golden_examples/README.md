# Evochia Golden Examples Registry

Golden examples teach structure, tone, visual hierarchy and projection patterns. They are **not policy authority** unless a separate canonical policy says so. Historical prices, VAT calculations, deposits or cancellation wording in a sample **does not control** current rates or current commercial policy.

| Golden family | Representative source | Controls | Does not control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Private Chef Proposal** | Private Household Chef / Villa Dinner proposal family | service-forward structure, summary hierarchy, inclusions/exclusions, warm closing, commercial-block presentation | current rates, universal staffing, tax profile |
| **Catering / Corporate Proposal** | Evochia catering/corporate proposal examples | formal-warm structure, service scope, operational clarity | current rates, legal terms unless separately approved |
| **Client Menu** | `Evochia_Sample_Menus.pdf`, Colette menu family | editorial menu hierarchy, themed evening/mood line, client-safe culinary language | recipe quantities, internal cost, current supplier availability |
| **Production Recipebook** | `Evochia_Colette_Recipebook_Compact_GR(1).pdf` | A4 production density, recipe blocks, shopping/production orientation | company pricing, client proposal copy |
| **Interactive Dining Master Product Plan** | Interactive Dining Master Business & Product Plan | product-development reasoning pattern, decision states, pilot/evidence/economics/capacity/governance structure | hardcoded assumptions for other products, automatic launch, current pricing |
| **Interactive Dining Client Flyer** | Interactive Dining color/BW flyer | compression of approved product into one-page client-safe value/flow/inclusions/practical/CTA | internal appendices, margin, product validation assumptions, universal deposit/current rates |

## Projection rule

`internal master/product/operations truth → approved client-safe projection → artifact renderer`

Never project INTERNAL margin, supplier comparison, opportunity cost, hidden assumptions or confidential annexes into a client artifact.
