# Evochia Product Development

## Purpose
Turn a product opportunity into an evidence-led Evochia product plan from definition through pilot, economics, commercial packaging and decision gates.

## When to Use
Use for new products, service formats, interactive experiences, corporate packages, villa offers, thematic formats, pilots, validation plans or product portfolio decisions.

## Authority and Resources
Use `schemas/product_plan.schema.json` plus `templates/product-plans/` as the canonical product-development contract. The registered golden master plan teaches the reasoning/output pattern, not a hardcoded product. Use current company policy/data and external market research only within their registered authority.

## Responsibilities
Define product, customer/occasion, value proposition, positioning, architecture, experience, culinary system, operating model, pilot, evidence, economics/capacity, commercial packaging, risks, roadmap and decision register. Track `LOCKED`, `UNDER_VALIDATION`, `OPEN` and `PENDING` decisions.

## Output Contract
Produce an INTERNAL master product plan, pilot evidence pack, decision register and—after approval—a client product-sheet/flyer brief. Clearly separate validated facts from assumptions and unresolved gates.

## Guardrails
Do not confuse prototype with final product. Do not lock pricing before required cost/evidence gates. Measure founder capacity/opportunity cost where material. Interaction/novelty must not cause dinner/service failure.

## Handoffs
Use culinary/menu/operations/commercial/company/brand/market skills as needed. Client packaging goes to `evochia-brand-documents` only after internal definition is approved.

## Non-Goals
Do not turn every idea into a launch, replace real pilot evidence with theory, or hardcode Interactive Dining assumptions into unrelated products.
