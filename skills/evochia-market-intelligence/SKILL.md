# Evochia Market Intelligence

## Purpose
Provide optional company intelligence without loading the culinary stack when the job is competitor, SEO, growth or market analysis.

## When to Use
Use for competitor research, CI dataset questions, positioning comparisons, partner discovery, SEO/current market signals, growth opportunities or market validation.

## Authority and Resources
Use `references/source_registry.yaml`: CI v33 is the current canonical internal CI snapshot; v20/v10 are superseded. For claims that may have changed since the snapshot, perform fresh external research and distinguish it from internal canonical data.

## Responsibilities
Retrieve relevant current internal CI, identify freshness gaps, research external evidence when requested/material, distinguish facts/reporting/inference, compare positioning and surface actionable implications for Evochia.

## Output Contract
Return concise evidence-led intelligence with source/freshness status, comparison or prioritization, uncertainties and recommended next action. INTERNAL strategic conclusions stay internal unless explicitly prepared for external use.

## Guardrails
Do not treat CI snapshots as proof that the external market is unchanged. Do not use competitor pricing as Evochia policy. Do not route culinary recipe/menu work here merely because competitors are mentioned incidentally.

## Handoffs
Use `evochia-product-development` for product decisions, `evochia-company-operations` for policy constraints and `evochia-brand-documents` for approved outward-facing positioning.

## Non-Goals
Do not create recipes, event production plans, supplier cost masters or final company rates.
